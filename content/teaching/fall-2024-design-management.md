## About this Course

This course covers the practical elements of business and strategic management, and how they relate to design. Through the course, we will be going over the entire business process, through the lens of 4 different situations in which designers operate. You will learn how to manage people, time, resources, projects, relationships and expectations, and how these elements can come together to build a strong business plan.

### Learning Objectives

1. Understand the different situations in which designers operate, and how the business and design process differs in each case.
2. Understand the basics of strategic management, as well as the basics of the different functions within an organization, and how they relate to design, or can be used as a design tool.
3. Understand how to build and present a compelling business case study.

## Course Mode

This course includes both synchronous and asynchronous components.

Throughout this term, plan to use Canvas Inbox as your primary way of communicating with me. The course consists of 4 different activities:

Readings
: The readings will vary based on the topic for the week, and will be introduced after each lecture. You don’t need to purchase a textbook in this class, all reading material will be provided.

Lectures
: The lectures will cover the theoretical elements of the course, and show how they can be used in (a design) practice. The lecture will also include a work-in-progress business case for you to understand how these concepts, frameworks and tools can be used in the context of design.

Case Studies
: Case studies are a way for you to think through a potential situation you might encounter, and the various ways you can react to it. They provide a safe space for you to practice the theories learned in the class and apply them to potential real world scenarios you may face as designers or managers, and then reflect on the effectiveness of the theories or tools.

Business Plan
: Throughout this class, you will have the opportunity to translate what you’ve learned into a full business plan for a business, product or service of your choosing; applying the different concepts and tools, and reflecting on their effectiveness.

## Grading

### Grading Criteria

Creativity
: The uniqueness of the reading reflection and case study responses.

Thoughtfulness
: How well each of the criteria presented were considered and addressed in the reading reflections/case study responses.

Rigor
: How well researched, and well considered the final responses were.

Structure
: How well your cases and arguments are structured, and whether you’re able to explain the reasoning and rationale for your decisions in a compelling manner.

Late Work Policy
: Assignments turned in late receive a 1% penalty per late day.
